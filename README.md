## Series Overview

**Title:** Final Fantasy XI: The Firesped Apocrypha  
**Season 1:** Children of Zvahl  

**Theme:** The loss of innocence, the bonds of unlikely friendship, and the trauma of being "saved."

### Core Premise
Before the catastrophic Northlands expedition, Raogrimm and Cornelia had a firstborn child: **Erge**. After Cornelia's death and resurrection as a Succubus by the Shadow Lord, she gave birth to twins: **Amelia** (Dark affinity) and **Celeste** (Light affinity).

- **Celeste** is imprisoned in Castle Zvahl, her Light suppressed by their corrupted mother.
- **Amelia** roams freely thanks to her Demonic signature, befriending lower-caste demons and clinging to stories of their older brother Erge who lives "where the sun actually touches the ground."

Amelia's ultimate goal: escape Zvahl, find Erge, and free Celeste.

This story expands the backstory of **Erge**, a character from the official [Lvl Up Xplorers](https://www.lvlupxp.com/) webcomic.

## Publishing Notes

This project is intended as a vertical-scroll or page-by-page webcomic.

**Recommended Platforms:**
- Webtoon Canvas / Tapas (for vertical format adaptation)
- Personal site / itch.io / GitHub Pages
- Discord / Reddit (r/FFXI, r/webcomics) for feedback

**Art Style:** AI-assisted anime-style panels with strong atmospheric lighting (cold blues/purples of Zvahl vs. warm sunlight motifs).
